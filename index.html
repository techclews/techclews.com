Claire Lewis - Event Technician

Live Event Technician · Audio, Photography, & Lighting

To me, it's more than just a gig; I want to help you bring your vision to life and create special moments

My primary discipline is Live Audio Engineering, with 4 years of event production experience. Throughout this time, I have had the opportunity to travel across the Southeast with various ensembles. While audio is my main focus, I have also explored lighting and live video, developing a broader understanding of the industry. I've also been able to assist with weddings, and I've contracted with local churches throughout the Mobile/Baldwin area. Beyond live production, I also love capturing events through photography. Since high school, I have captured a variety of events, and I enjoy preserving beautiful and unique moments. As a senior in college, I am beginning to establish myself in the professional industry. I am passionate about the technical and creative aspects of production and love bringing events to life through sound, lighting, and visual storytelling.
